What We Do

Transaction Execution Counsel

Transaction Structuring

We design transaction architectures that align commercial objectives, regulatory frameworks, and funding requirements from the outset. Every structure we build is engineered to withstand scrutiny across multiple jurisdictions and approval processes.

Our approach begins with a deep understanding of the commercial intent behind each deal. We map the regulatory landscape, identify structural constraints, and engineer solutions that satisfy all stakeholders while preserving the economic substance of the transaction.

The result is a deal framework that moves through approvals efficiently because the hard questions have already been answered in the structure itself.

  • Cross-border holding and financing structures
  • Joint venture and consortium arrangements
  • Tax-efficient capital deployment frameworks
  • Multi-party governance and control mechanisms
  • Regulatory-compliant investment vehicles

Regulatory De-Risking

Regulatory failure is the leading cause of deal collapse in emerging markets. We anticipate approval requirements, licensing conditions, and compliance obligations before they become obstacles, building the path to clearance into the transaction design.

Our regulatory intelligence spans central bank approvals, sector-specific licensing, competition clearances, and foreign investment restrictions. We maintain current knowledge of how regulators interpret and apply their mandates in practice, not just on paper.

  • Central bank and foreign exchange approvals
  • Sector licensing and permit sequencing
  • Competition and merger control filings
  • Anti-money laundering and sanctions compliance
  • Data protection and cross-border data transfer

Execution Through Close

A well-structured deal still requires disciplined execution. We drive transactions from structuring through every condition precedent, regulatory clearance, and documentation milestone to financial close. Our execution methodology eliminates the gaps where deals stall.

We coordinate across counterparties, advisors, and regulators with a focus on maintaining momentum. Every workstream has clear ownership, defined timelines, and escalation protocols that keep the transaction on track.

Our role does not end at signing. We manage the path from execution to completion, ensuring that conditions are satisfied, funds flow as intended, and closing deliverables are in order.

  • Transaction management and workstream coordination
  • Condition precedent tracking and satisfaction
  • Closing mechanics and funds flow management
  • Post-closing integration and compliance handover

"We are engaged where failure is costly and certainty is non-negotiable."
