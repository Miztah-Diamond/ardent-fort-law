Ardent Fort Law is a specialist transaction law firm advising on complex, regulated, and capital-intensive transactions across emerging markets.

We support international investors, DFIs, private equity, venture capital, and project sponsors deploying capital into Nigeria and other emerging markets — where regulatory certainty, execution discipline, and local fluency are critical to outcomes.

We do not practice general law.
We structure, de-risk, and execute transactions.
